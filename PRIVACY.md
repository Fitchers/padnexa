# Padnexa privacy policy

_Last updated: November 8, 2026_

Padnexa does not collect, store or send any personal information. It has no accounts, analytics, telemetry, advertising or crash reporting.

## What stays on your computer

Padnexa saves your profiles and settings in your Windows user folder: controller lighting, trigger effects, mappings, language and similar options. They never leave your PC.

To show the controller's state, Padnexa reads its input on your computer: buttons, sticks, motion sensors, touchpad, battery and microphone. This data goes only to the games and programs you choose:

- the virtual controller;
- the local DSX mod, DSU and OSC servers, which listen on 127.0.0.1 unless you change the address.

## Network access

Padnexa connects to the internet only in these cases:

- **Driver setup.** When you press **Install** for a driver (usbip-win2, HidHide, ViGEmBus), Padnexa downloads the official installer from GitHub.
- **Updates.** The version installed outside the Microsoft Store checks GitHub for new releases. The Microsoft Store version is updated by the Store.

These requests contain no information about you. GitHub receives the usual technical data of any download, such as your IP address, under [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
